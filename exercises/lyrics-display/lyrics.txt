Morning light spills through the window pane
Coffee steam rises like a lazy refrain
The radio hums a half-remembered tune
Another day arriving way too soon

Footsteps echo down the empty hall
Shadows stretching long against the wall
A melody that no one else can hear
Keeps playing softly somewhere near

We chase the notes across the fading sky
Hoping that the music never dies
Every verse a bridge to somewhere new
Every chorus bringing me to you

The evening falls and wraps the world in blue
Stars appear like notes we never knew
A final chord hangs gently in the air
A song that lingers everywhere
