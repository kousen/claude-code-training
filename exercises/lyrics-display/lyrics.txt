Morning light filters through the window pane,
Coffee steam curling like a lazy refrain.
The cat stretches out on the kitchen floor,
Another day begins, just like before.

Footsteps echo down the empty hall,
Shadows dancing on the western wall.
A melody hums from a distant room,
Chasing away the afternoon gloom.

Stars appear when the sun goes down,
Silence settles over the sleeping town.
The porch light flickers, then holds its glow,
And the night moves on, steady and slow.
